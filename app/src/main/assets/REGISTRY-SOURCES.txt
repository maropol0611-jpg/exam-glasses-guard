KoriyoSCAN identity registry data
Retrieved 2026-11-16 (Asia/Seoul).

mac-vendors.tsv: 24-bit MA-L assignments from IEEE Registration Authority.
Source: https://standards-oui.ieee.org/oui/oui.csv
Columns retained: Assignment and Organization Name. Addresses omitted.
Only globally administered unicast addresses are queried. BLE matches are
registration candidates: a random BLE address can coincide with a registered OUI.
No MA-M/MA-S lookup or model inference from an OUI is performed.

bluetooth-companies.tsv: Bluetooth SIG Company Identifiers.
Source: https://bitbucket.org/bluetooth-SIG/public/raw/main/assigned_numbers/company_identifiers/company_identifiers.yaml
Official index: https://www.bluetooth.com/specifications/assigned-numbers/
Columns retained: decimal company identifier and company name.
Identifiers indicate the company responsible for manufacturer-specific advertising
data; they do not certify the physical device brand or model.

The files contain factual registry entries and retain official organization
names. The files ship with the app; scans and address lookups do not contact a
remote lookup service. Refresh from official sources for newer registrations.
